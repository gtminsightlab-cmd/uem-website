## Initial Approved Decisions

### D-001 — Multi-Tenant From Day One
**Status:** Approved  
LaunchOS production architecture is multi-tenant from first schema.

### D-002 — Demo Uses Production Architecture
**Status:** Approved  
Demo is a seeded tenant running real rules/workflows. No demo-only business logic.

### D-003 — Service-Led SaaS
**Status:** Approved  
Initial value is software + senior operators + expert network. Pure self-service is not the initial product.

### D-004 — Vendor Neutrality
**Status:** Approved  
LaunchOS represents the manufacturer, not vendors.

### D-005 — First Wedge
**Status:** Approved  
First U.S. launches for emerging/global manufacturers.

### D-006 — Control-Tower UX
**Status:** Approved  
Command Center is decision-centric, not database/dashboard-centric.

### D-007 — AI Must Detect Gaps
**Status:** Approved  
AI is required to identify missing evidence and escalate rather than fabricate a decision-grade answer.


### D-008 — Founding-Team Pharma Launch Experience Is Canonical
**Status:** Approved  
Any prior framing that LaunchOS lacks pharma launch experience is superseded. Sharon O'Dell's 25 years in pharmaceutical commercial leadership, multiple product launches, 200+ field-rep hires, 30+ district-manager hires, and 1,000+ representative interviews are canonical product context.

### D-009 — Field/Organization Methodology Has a Founding Operator
**Status:** Approved  
Sharon is the primary founding operator source for early methodology covering organization design, hiring, certification, manager spans, recruiting sequencing, CRM/call planning, launch readiness, and field execution. Methodology must be versioned/configurable, not hard-coded.

### D-010 — Market Access Is the First Expert-Council Anchor Gap
**Status:** Approved  
The first anchor expert recruited for LaunchOS should be a senior market-access / payer-strategy leader to close the most material expertise gap currently identified.


### D-011 — Amplity Experience Informs CSO/Outsourced Field Methodology
**Status:** Approved  
Sharon's approximately four years at Amplity, including scaling a contract field organization from roughly 30 to 80 representatives and an estimated contract value increase from approximately $5M to $15M, is canonical product input for outsourced field-force design, CSO economics, vendor selection, scaling, recruiting, manager structure, governance, and deployment methodology.

Dollar values remain user-estimated and must be labeled as estimates unless independently verified.

### D-012 — Amplity Is Not a Preferred Partner
**Status:** Approved  
LaunchOS remains vendor-neutral and should not recommend Amplity as a preferred CSO partner. Sharon's Amplity experience is used as buyer-side insight; future field-force partner shortlists should emphasize qualified competitors.

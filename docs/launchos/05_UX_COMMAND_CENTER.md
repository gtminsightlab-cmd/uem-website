# UX & Command Center

## UX Rule

Do not build primary navigation around database entities.

Users think:
- Are we ready?
- What changed?
- What is late?
- What needs a decision?
- What happens next?
- What are we missing?

## Command Center

Header:
- manufacturer
- product
- indication
- PDUFA / launch date
- days to launch
- launch archetype

Executive scorecards:
- Overall Readiness
- Critical Risks
- Decisions Due
- Critical Path
- Organization Readiness
- Vendor Readiness
- Data Readiness
- Field Readiness
- Access Readiness
- Distribution Readiness

Primary element:
### What requires your attention this week?

Each item:
- what changed,
- why it matters,
- evidence,
- confidence,
- expected impact,
- owner,
- due date,
- actions.

Actions:
- Review Decision
- Assign
- Ask Expert
- Model Scenario
- Show Evidence

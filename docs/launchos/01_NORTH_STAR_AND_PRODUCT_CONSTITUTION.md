# North Star & Product Constitution

## North Star

> Help pharmaceutical commercial leaders design smarter launches, identify failure earlier, and make better commercialization decisions — whether it is their company’s first U.S. launch or their tenth.

## Primary Customer

First-time U.S. launch manufacturer:
- Phase III / NDA / BLA / PDUFA / newly approved,
- U.S. rights retained,
- small U.S. commercial organization,
- 6–24 months from launch,
- specialty/focused HCP universe preferred,
- capital-conscious,
- needs to determine what to build, buy, rent, integrate, and monitor.

## Primary User Questions

LaunchOS must continuously help answer:
- Are we ready?
- What is missing?
- What is late?
- What decision is required?
- What happens if we wait?
- What assumptions are weak?
- What should we build, buy, rent, or delay?
- Where is commercial friction developing?
- What should leadership pay attention to this week?
- Does the system know enough, or should it ask an expert?

## Product Principles

1. Multi-tenant from first production schema.
2. Security before integrations.
3. UI follows decisions, not tables.
4. Methodology drives product behavior.
5. AI must identify missing information.
6. AI must not invent confidence.
7. Expert escalation is first-class.
8. Expert access is scoped and time-bound.
9. Rules are versioned and auditable.
10. No tenant-confidential data trains shared models.
11. No cross-tenant retrieval or semantic search.
12. Integrate established pharma infrastructure; do not recreate it.
13. Minimize PHI in V1.
14. Every recommendation retains evidence and confidence.
15. Every decision retains assumptions and rationale.
16. Every feature maps to Feature → Advantage → Benefit → Payoff.
17. The Command Center prioritizes decisions and consequences, not vanity dashboards.
18. Demo data is seed data only — never application logic.
19. All business methodology must be configurable.
20. Build vertical slices that demonstrate real end-to-end value.

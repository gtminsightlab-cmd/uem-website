# Asterion Demo Tenant — Scenario Guide

Asterion BioPharma is a **fictional** Seoul-based emerging manufacturer preparing its first U.S. specialty launch.

The demo exists to prove production architecture.

## Scenario
- ~14 months to PDUFA
- 4,800 target HCPs
- 620 preliminary Tier-1 targets
- 7-person current U.S. commercial team
- 35–50 field reps under consideration
- Internal vs CSO vs Hybrid unresolved
- payer research incomplete
- CRM in evaluation
- distribution strategy defined but contracts incomplete
- FLSM sequencing risk
- stale payer evidence

## Must demonstrate
1. Live readiness calculation.
2. Insufficient-evidence response to “How many reps?”
3. Live rule firing.
4. Expert request.
5. Milestone/dependency impact.
6. Build/Buy/Rent comparison.
7. Live Command Center.
8. Live executive report.

## Forbidden
No code may branch on the demo company, product, or slug.
No static readiness scores, brief text, AI outputs, expert answers, or rule results.

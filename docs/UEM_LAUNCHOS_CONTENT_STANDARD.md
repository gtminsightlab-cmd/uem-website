# UEM + LaunchOS Public Content Standard

**Status:** Current website content authority  
**Purpose:** Keep public UEM messaging aligned with verified LaunchOS product and founder context.

## Brand and product relationship

- **Upper Echelon Management (UEM)** is the operator-led commercialization firm.
- **LaunchOS** is the AI-native, multi-tenant U.S. pharmaceutical commercialization operating system being developed as part of UEM's work.
- UEM supplies operator leadership and scoped expert judgment. LaunchOS is intended to make methodology, evidence, risks, decisions, dependencies, and next actions configurable and visible.
- Until name/trademark clearance and product release decisions are complete, public copy must describe LaunchOS as **in development** and must not imply general commercial availability.

## Approved product language

North Star:

> Help pharmaceutical commercial leaders design smarter launches, identify failure earlier, and make better commercialization decisions—whether it is their company's first U.S. launch or their tenth.

Primary wedge:

- emerging U.S. biopharma and global manufacturers preparing a first U.S. launch;
- typically 6–24 months from launch, with retained U.S. rights and a small U.S. commercial organization;
- specialty or focused HCP universes are preferred, but UEM and LaunchOS are not limited to one therapeutic area.

LaunchOS may be described as:

- a manufacturer-side decision layer;
- a U.S. Commercialization Office;
- evidence-aware and designed to identify missing information;
- configurable and versioned rather than hard-coded;
- multi-tenant by design;
- vendor-neutral;
- service-led software that combines operator knowledge, expert input, deterministic rules, and AI.

LaunchOS must not be described as:

- a generic project manager, CRM, dashboard collection, or ChatGPT wrapper;
- a replacement for Veeva, Salesforce, IQVIA, MMIT, HUBs, distributors, CSOs, MLR, or PV systems;
- validated to predict launch success;
- generally available or enterprise-proven while the Conviction Prototype is in development.

## Claims removed from the prior site pending evidence

The following claims are not approved for public use unless the owner supplies evidence and confirms permission:

- specific drug/product launch histories;
- "top-quartile" territory or launch outcomes;
- client retention or discontinuation improvements;
- near-zero-to-tens-of-millions client revenue claims;
- zero-turnover hiring outcomes;
- invented anonymized client case studies;
- fixed consulting prices, retainer levels, or performance bonuses;
- a proprietary UEM Fit Score or predictive score;
- guaranteed 24-hour personal responses;
- a 13-report library being "available now" when download/access is not wired;
- synthetic group imagery presented as the UEM team.

## Visual truth standard

- Synthetic editorial scenes may illustrate the kinds of people, roles, and working situations involved in commercialization.
- Label synthetic people imagery as an **illustrative working scene** wherever a reasonable viewer could otherwise read it as documentary evidence.
- Never present synthetic people as UEM founders, employees, experts, clients, or participants in a real engagement.
- Use real approved portraits for named people. Do not generate replacements or imply a synthetic likeness is the person.
- Prefer credible work in progress over staged handshakes, generic laboratory theater, national stereotypes, or decorative "AI" imagery.

## Content-review test

Before publishing a claim, answer:

1. Which source authorizes it?
2. Is it a fact, estimate, aspiration, candidate methodology, or product-in-development statement?
3. Could a buyer reasonably mistake it for a verified result or currently working feature?
4. Does it preserve UEM/LaunchOS vendor neutrality and evidence honesty?
5. Does it reveal confidential client or former-employer information?

If the answer is unclear, add it to `docs/UEM_OPEN_CONTENT_QUESTIONS.md` and omit it from public copy.
